About

I am an MEng Mathematics and Computer Science student at Imperial College London (September 2023–June 2027, expected). This is a four-year, full-time undergraduate integrated master’s programme taught jointly by the Department of Computing and the Department of Mathematics.

My research focuses on evaluating and improving AI agents: separating decision quality from system design, modelling populations in LLM-based surveys, and studying memory and collaboration in multi-agent systems. I have conducted research at CKGSB and Zhejiang University.

Manuscripts under review

From System Performance to Decision Attribution in Agent Evaluation

Hongrui Chen, Yingjie Zhang, Jiarong Xu

Under review at ICLR

Matched Policy Intervention (MPI) isolates the contribution of an agent’s decision policy by holding available actions fixed and randomizing which policy recommendation is executed. Experiments across CausalGame, CARE, and a discrete-action CausaLab adaptation show how system-level gains can disappear when decision affordances are matched.

First-author project; developed with Prof. Yingjie Zhang and Prof. Jiarong Xu.

OpenReview · Code

Population Modeling Should Be a First-Order Design Problem in LLM-Based Survey Simulation

Hou-Wan Long, Wen Wang, Hongrui Chen, Tianshu Sun

Under review at AAAI

Studies why LLM survey simulations misrepresent target populations. We formalize target, implicit-model, and mediator populations, and evaluate population-structured inference using 12,000 social-media posts and 138,000 survey responses.

Selected research and projects

TRIAGE-Bench

A 2,000-item benchmark of jurisdictional, temporal, regulatory, and polypharmacy evidence conflicts. A 2 × 2 Hint × Source design and 56,000 responses from seven models help distinguish source-selection failures from action-mapping failures.

Public benchmark on Hugging Face

Memory Application Channels in Dynamic Multi-Agent Systems

At Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Zemin Liu in Prof. Fei Wu’s group, I study how agents apply memory through advice, rewrites, and artifacts, tracking exposure, adoption, and outcomes on TerminalBench.

Causal Spatiotemporal Deep Emulator for Secondary Motion

Advised by Prof. Jin Huang, I built a causal graph-network emulator for soft-body simulation. A causal-cone ablation across ten unseen character–motion pairs reduced MSE by 66% on average with 0.07 ms overhead.

AI Quotient Assessment and Autonomous Research Agents

At CKGSB’s AI and Intelligent Industry Lab, I built executable human–AI benchmarks with deterministic scoring and self-validation, and research-agent workflows spanning topic discovery, experiments, manuscript drafting, and iterative review.

Metric-Mode: Autonomous ML Agent

Technical lead for a Kotlin/Koog agent that automates the tabular-ML lifecycle, from conversational requirements to training and PDF/HTML reports. Winner of the IC Hack 2026 JetBrains Kotlin Koog track.
